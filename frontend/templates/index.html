Core Innovation

More Than a Dashboard.
A Living Digital Twin.

🧬

Digital Twin Engine

Every student gets a dynamic JSON simulation state — updated after every quiz, click, and session — modeling mastery, forgetting, pace, confidence, and motivation.

📉

Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve

The system predicts when each concept will fade from memory using stability decay models and schedules targeted reviews before retention drops below 65%.

🔮

Simulation Mode

"What if I study 30 minutes a day for 2 weeks?" The digital twin projects a 3-scenario forecast — best case, expected, and worst case — before you commit to a plan.

⚠️

Predictive Risk Alerts

Instead of "You scored 40%", TwinLearn warns "You're likely to struggle with Calculus Integration within 12 days unless prerequisite concepts are reinforced."

🤖

AI-Adaptive Quizzing

Bayesian Knowledge Tracing personalizes questions to your current mastery level, dynamically updating probabilities with slip and guess rates.

👩‍🏫

Teacher & Parent Ecosystem

Teachers monitor a class-wide risk matrix with early-warning flags. Parents receive plain-language summaries and supportive intervention recommendations.
